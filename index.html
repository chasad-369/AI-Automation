Good to know

A few answers
before we begin.

What kinds of businesses can use automation?

Almost any business with recurring admin, handoffs, follow-up, or information moving between tools can benefit. I start with your process, not a one-size-fits-all template.

Do I need new software?

Not necessarily. A good automation often connects and improves the tools you already use. If a different tool would make more sense, that is part of the planning conversation.

Can AI be part of my workflow?

Yes. AI can help with tasks such as sorting incoming requests, summarising information, preparing drafts, and finding the right next step — with the right checks built in.

What happens in the first conversation?

We will talk through the work you want to simplify, the systems you use today, and what a useful outcome would look like. From there, I can identify the best first step.
